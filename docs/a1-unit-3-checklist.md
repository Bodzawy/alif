## Deviations and notes

- هِنْدِي / هُنُود: the book's pictures show Native Americans with feather
  headdress; German: „Ureinwohner Amerikas“.
- ظ separate form: the book prints „وعَاظ“; Alif writes وُعَّاظ (plural of وَاعِظ).
- T3 of ه and ط: the book leaves most lines blank (the teacher dictates the
  words again); Alif dictates the words of T2 in T2's order.
- عَيْن („Auge“) is evaluated by the existing letter rule „عين“ (same sounds).
- The conversation „مَنْ هُوَ؟“: the book does not say who asks; here the woman
  asks, the man answers.
- Lektion 6 Übung 5: the book numbers the lines 1–5, 7 (no 6) – kept.
