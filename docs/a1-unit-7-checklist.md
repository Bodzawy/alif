## Notes
- Prices are spoken as number words (src/lib/arabic/numbers.ts: bigNumberWord, priceWords); a comma before three digits is a thousands separator (١٥,٠٣٠), before two digits it separates piasters (١٥,٦٠). The blouse in تدريب ٦ is printed „١٤٥,٥٠٠“ and read as 145,50 Pfund (noted).
- p. 242 تدريب ٢ and p. 221 تدريب ٣: the book gives no complete key; Alif's choices are noted on the pages.
- أَلْف („tausend“) is evaluated by the letter rule „ألف“ (same sounds).
- Pictures: unit-7/ (clothes, electrical goods, colours, rooms, furniture, avatars).
